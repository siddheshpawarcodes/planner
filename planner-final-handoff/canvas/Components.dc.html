Each block is one Flutter widget. The states shown are all of its states: if a state isn’t here, it doesn’t exist. Specimens are drawn at 1:1 in the dark theme, the default, with light-theme checks where colour changes meaning.

Every interactive widget getsDefault, Hover (desktop, web), Pressed, Focused (2px tx ring, 2px offset), Loading, Success, Error, Disabled and, where it applies, Completed.Targets are 44 × 44 minimum. Every widget has a Semantics label, written out in its spec.

PlannerOrb

widgets/voice/planner_orb.dart (CustomPainter + Ticker)
{{ o.k }} {{ o.d }} {{ o.p }}

Foreground only. The wake phrase “Hey Planner” is armed only while Today is the visible route and the app is resumed. On pause, route change, sheet or review, the recogniser stops. There is no background service.

Amplitude. listening amp = clamp(RMS × 7) × 0.95 + 0.12 from the recorder’s amplitude stream at 30 Hz. Peaks over 0.62 spawn a ripple, at most one every 420ms.

Core colour. The core is the category of the current task. During voice it switches to the category of what is being scheduled. With no current task it is warm white.
